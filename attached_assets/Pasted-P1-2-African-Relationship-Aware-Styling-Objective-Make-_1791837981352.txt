P1.2 — African Relationship-Aware Styling

Objective
Make Amodka's Styling Intelligence correctly consume existing Phase A garment relationships when constructing outfit recommendations.

This is a focused relationship-awareness capability, not a recommendation-engine rewrite.

The goal is to make confirmed African coordinated/layered relationships materially useful to styling while preserving the existing v3.7 engine unchanged.

Existing implementation
- P0.1 canonical wardrobe semantics is complete and production-validated.
- P0.2 canonical wardrobe → Styling Intelligence boundary is complete and production-validated.
- P1.1 Traditional Styling Context is complete and production-validated.
- Phase A garment groups are production-approved.
- Recommendation Engine v3.7 is FROZEN.
- Existing African taxonomy is unchanged.
- The African Fashion Capability Audit and Stylist Independence Audit are the source of truth.
- Do not perform another whole-repository audit.

Core product principle

A garment relationship is not the same thing as an individual garment attribute.

Examples:
- buba + iro may be two independently wearable garments with a confirmed coordinated_set relationship.
- two garments may belong to a coordinated set without being interchangeable with arbitrary wardrobe items.
- layered garments may have an explicit layered relationship even when exact layer order is unknown.

Styling Intelligence must preserve these relationships rather than flattening them into unrelated WardrobeItems.

Required relationship types

Consume the existing Phase A relationship types only:
- coordinated_set
- multi_item
- layered

Do not add new relationship types.

Do not modify Phase A schema, migrations, RLS, persistence lifecycle, or group creation.

Relationship authority

Treat existing user-confirmed/authoritative group relationships as styling evidence.

Do not infer a relationship merely because:
- colours match
- patterns match
- subtypes appear compatible
- garments are culturally associated
- Gemini classified them similarly

AI/provider output must not silently create a durable relationship.

Existing Phase A group data is the source of relationship truth.

Coordinated sets

For a confirmed coordinated_set:

- preserve the relationship during candidate construction
- allow the set to be considered as a coherent styling unit where appropriate
- do not automatically separate its members into unrelated candidates
- do not require every coordinated-set member to appear in every possible outfit unless the relationship/context requires it
- do not assume matching print/colour means the pieces must always be worn together
- do not fabricate missing members

For African examples such as buba + iro, a confirmed relationship should be materially preferable to treating the pieces as unrelated garments when the styling context supports the set.

Traditional context

When the user has selected Traditional:

- preserve confirmed African coordinated sets
- allow the set to contribute coherently to the outfit
- prefer an owned confirmed coordinated African set over arbitrary independently paired pieces when both are otherwise viable
- continue allowing appropriate Western complements
- do not require every outfit to contain a coordinated African set
- do not infer cultural requirements not explicitly represented

Do not introduce:
- Nigerian wedding mode
- church mode
- aso-ebi role
- required gele
- ethnic/cultural identity inference
- authenticity classification

Layered relationships

For layered:

- preserve the relationship
- do not invent layer order when it is unknown
- do not treat the members as arbitrary unrelated garments
- retain enough relationship information for future context-aware styling

Do not build a new layer-order inference engine in this task.

Multi-item relationships

For multi_item:

- preserve the relationship and membership
- prevent accidental semantic flattening
- do not assume coordinated styling unless relationship_type explicitly says coordinated_set
- do not invent compatibility requirements

Styling projection

Extend the P0.2 Styling Intelligence contract only as necessary to expose relationship-aware styling information.

Prefer an additive typed projection.

Do not duplicate relationship data into wardrobe rows.

Do not create a second relationship table.

Do not create persistent derived relationship records.

The canonical database remains the source of truth.

Candidate construction

Identify the narrowest existing candidate-construction boundary where relationship information can be consumed.

Relationship-aware behaviour should happen BEFORE final v3.7 evaluation where necessary, so v3.7 can continue evaluating valid candidate combinations using its existing scoring.

Do not change v3.7 scoring/ranking formulas.

Do not add relationship bonuses directly into v3.7.

Do not modify affinity/pair-affinity algorithms.

Do not modify weather logic.

Do not modify daily rotation.

Do not create a parallel scoring system.

The relationship layer may:
- preserve a valid set
- prevent an invalid/unwanted split
- construct a candidate using related members
- make a relationship-aware candidate available to the existing engine

The existing v3.7 engine remains responsible for its normal evaluation/ranking.

African relationship behaviour

The implementation must work for African wardrobe relationships without hard-coding individual cultural garments.

For example:

buba + iro
with coordinated_set
→ can reach the recommendation engine as a coherent candidate.

But the implementation must NOT become:

if subtype === "buba" then find iro.

Relationship data, not subtype heuristics, must drive this.

This distinction is critical.

Mixed African + Western styling

Continue supporting appropriate Western complements.

Example:
confirmed buba + iro
+
owned appropriate shoes/bag
→ valid Traditional outfit candidate.

Do not force all members of an outfit to belong to the same cultural category.

Do not introduce an African-only mode.

Generic styling

Relationship awareness must not unnecessarily alter generic recommendations.

A confirmed relationship may still be consumed where relevant, but existing generic behaviour should remain stable unless relationship information genuinely changes candidate construction.

Existing generic fixtures must remain unchanged where no relationship is involved.

Traditional + relationship integration

Use the existing P1.1 Traditional context.

Do not create a second Traditional pipeline.

The intended architecture is:

Canonical wardrobe
        ↓
P0.2 Styling semantic boundary
        ↓
Phase A relationships
        ↓
P1.1 Traditional context when selected
        ↓
Relationship-aware candidate construction
        ↓
existing v3.7 evaluation/ranking
        ↓
recommendation

User experience

Make the benefit visible, but do not redesign the outfit screen.

Where a recommendation uses a confirmed coordinated set, the UI should preserve/communicate that relationship naturally if the current outfit-card architecture supports it.

Do not add a large new UI section merely to expose relationship metadata.

The user should experience:
"Am odka understood these pieces belong together."

not:
"Am odka showed me database relationship metadata."

If existing outfit cards already have a suitable presentation surface, reuse it.

Do not introduce new terminology such as "relationship_type" to users.

Determinism

Relationship-aware candidate construction must be:
- deterministic
- explainable
- reproducible
- provider-free
- based on canonical wardrobe + existing Phase A relationships + explicit styling context

No Gemini calls.
No LLM calls.
No external provider calls.

Unknown values remain unknown.

Do not fill missing garment attributes from relationship metadata.

Important invariants

1. Individual garment truth remains unchanged.
2. Group truth remains unchanged.
3. Shared group attributes never overwrite individual garment attributes.
4. A coordinated_set relationship does not imply identical attributes.
5. A layered relationship does not imply known layer order.
6. A multi_item relationship does not imply coordinated styling.
7. A relationship does not imply cultural authenticity.
8. A relationship does not imply required headwear.
9. No new durable relationship is inferred by this feature.

Success criterion

Amodka no longer treats confirmed African garment relationships as merely database metadata.

A confirmed relationship such as buba + iro can materially influence outfit construction through Styling Intelligence, reach the existing v3.7 evaluation machinery as a coherent candidate, remain compatible with appropriate Western complements, and remain fully deterministic and user-truth-driven.

The implementation must prove this with controlled tests while leaving the frozen v3.7 engine itself unchanged.
