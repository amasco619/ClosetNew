# Terms of Service

## Using Amodka

Amodka supports guest use and signed-in accounts. Guest wardrobe information is kept locally. Creating an account can sync eligible guest items through the app’s guest-to-account flow; signing in to an existing account does not migrate those guest items.

## Wardrobe features

Users can add garment photos, review and edit garment details, and receive outfit suggestions based on wardrobe items and profile preferences. Recommendations are generated in the app from the available wardrobe and context.

## Photo processing

Garment photos may be processed by Google Gemini for classification and, when selected, by PhotoRoom for background removal. These signed-in image-processing routes require recorded AI-processing permission.

## Account controls

Users can edit wardrobe details, remove wardrobe items and request account deletion from the app. The deletion flow also clears the account’s defined cloud records and local app data when its steps complete successfully.
