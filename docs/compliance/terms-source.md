## 1. Account

- Amodka requires users to create an account (email + password) to access the full wardrobe management and recommendation features.
- A limited "Guest" mode allows up to 8 wardrobe items without account creation. Guest data is stored locally only and is not recoverable if the app is deleted or the device is replaced.
- Users must provide a valid email address. Email confirmation is encouraged for account recovery.
- Account sharing is not supported.
- Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their login credentials.
- Amodka reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate acceptable use terms (to be defined by solicitor).

**Age requirement:**
- **Nigeria:** NDPA 2023 treats persons under 18 as children requiring special protection. **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — whether a minimum age of 18 (or lower, subject to parental consent) should be set.
- **UK:** ICO Children's Code may apply. **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** — minimum age and age verification mechanism to be determined by solicitor.
- **Do not specify a minimum age in the Terms until the legal/product decision is made.**

## 2. Acceptable Use

The following behaviour is prohibited:

- Uploading images that are not photographs of garments (e.g. other people's photographs, illegal or offensive images)
- Using automated tools or scripts to call classification, background-removal, or other API endpoints
- Attempting to reverse-engineer, extract, or copy Amodka's recommendation engine or AI pipeline
- Attempting to access another user's account, wardrobe data, or profile
- Using the service to infringe third-party intellectual property
- Attempting to bypass or circumvent free-tier limits or premium feature restrictions
- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent quotas
- Using the service for any purpose that violates applicable law (including Nigerian law, UK law, or the laws of the user's jurisdiction)

## 3. AI-Generated Recommendations

Key disclosures that must be in published Terms:

- Outfit recommendations are generated by Amodka's proprietary recommendation engine using the user's wardrobe data and profile preferences.
- Garment classification (category, colour, fabric, fit, etc.) is performed by Google Gemini, an AI model operated by Google LLC.
- Background removal (optional) is performed by PhotoRoom, operated by PhotoRoom SAS.
- AI classifications may be inaccurate. Users can edit any classification after upload.
- Recommendations are suggestions only. Amodka makes no warranty about fashion suitability, professional appropriateness, or any other outcome.
- Amodka does not guarantee that any specific outfit will be recommended on any given occasion.

**Nigeria-specific disclosure:** The Terms must be written in plain language as required by FCCPC guidance. Technical descriptions of AI must be accessible to a general Nigerian consumer audience.

## 4. Wardrobe Content

- Users retain ownership of photographs they upload.
- By uploading photographs, users grant Amodka a licence to process those photographs using AI services (Google Gemini, PhotoRoom) for the purpose of generating outfit recommendations. **The scope and duration of this licence must be drafted by a solicitor.**
- Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to upload any photograph.
- Amodka processes garment images only. The app is not designed to process images of people (no Virtual Try-On feature currently).
- Users can delete any wardrobe item at any time. Deletion removes the image from Supabase Storage.
- Account deletion removes all uploaded photographs from Amodka's storage systems.

## 6. Cancellation and Account Termination

- Users may delete their account at any time via the in-app profile screen.
- Account deletion removes:
  - All wardrobe images from Supabase Storage
  - All wardrobe metadata from the database
  - All wear logs, affinity signals, and preference data
  - All profile data
  - The Supabase authentication record
  - All locally cached data on the device (AsyncStorage cleared)
- Account deletion is irreversible. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
- **Operator pending action:** A web-based deletion URL must be implemented before Google Play (and Apple, if applicable) submission.
- For subscription cancellation: cancellation stops auto-renewal but premium access continues until the end of the billing period. Amodka does not pro-rate refunds for unused subscription time.
