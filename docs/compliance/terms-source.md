## 1. Account

- Amodka requires users to create an account (email + password) to access the full wardrobe management and recommendation features.
- A limited "Guest" mode exists allowing up to 8 wardrobe items without account creation. Guest data is stored locally only and is not recoverable if the app is deleted or the device is lost.
- Users must provide a valid email address. Email confirmation is encouraged for account recovery.
- Account sharing is not designed for and not supported.
- Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their credentials.
- Amodka reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate acceptable use terms (to be defined by legal).
- **TBD — BUSINESS/LEGAL:** Age requirement. The product does not currently restrict access by age. Legal must confirm whether a minimum age applies and what verification is required.

## 2. Acceptable Use

Behaviour that must be prohibited in the published Terms:

- Uploading images that are not photographs of garments (e.g., other people's photos, sensitive or illegal images)
- Attempting to reverse-engineer or extract AI models
- Using automated tools to spam the classification or background-removal endpoints
- Attempting to access other users' accounts or data
- Using the service to infringe third-party intellectual property rights
- Attempting to bypass premium feature restrictions
- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent free-tier limits

---

## 3. AI-Generated Recommendations

Key factual product behaviour that must be disclosed:

- Outfit recommendations are generated by Amodka's proprietary recommendation engine using the user's wardrobe data and profile preferences.
- Garment classification (category, colour, fabric, fit, etc.) is performed by Google Gemini, an AI model operated by Google LLC.
- Background removal is performed by PhotoRoom, operated by PhotoRoom SAS.
- AI classifications may be inaccurate. Users can edit any classification after upload.
- Recommendations are suggestions only. Amodka makes no warranty about fashion suitability, professional appropriateness, or any other outcome.
- Amodka does not guarantee that any specific outfit will be recommended on any given day.

---

## 4. Wardrobe Content

- Users retain ownership of photographs they upload.
- By uploading photographs, users grant Amodka a licence to process those photographs using AI services (Google Gemini, PhotoRoom) for the purpose of generating outfit recommendations. **The scope of this licence must be drafted by a solicitor.**
- Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to upload any photograph they submit.
- Amodka processes garment images only. The app is not designed to receive or process images of people.
- Users can delete any wardrobe item at any time. Deletion removes the image from Supabase Storage.

## 6. Cancellation and Account Termination

- Users may delete their account at any time via the in-app profile screen.
- Account deletion removes: all wardrobe images from Supabase Storage, all wardrobe metadata, all wear logs, all profile data, and the Supabase Auth user record.
- Account deletion is irreversible. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
- **Outstanding:** Google Play requires an external web-based deletion URL in addition to the in-app deletion route. This must be implemented before Google Play submission.

## 9. Service Availability and Limitations

- Amodka does not guarantee 100% uptime.
- The free tier limits: 30 wardrobe items, 2 outfit recommendations per scenario per day.
- The guest tier limits: 8 wardrobe items, 1 outfit recommendation per scenario per day.
- AI classification results are provided "as is" and may be corrected by the user.
- Rate limiting is applied to AI classification and background removal endpoints to prevent abuse. Users who trigger rate limits may experience temporary service unavailability.
